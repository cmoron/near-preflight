near-preflight

The journal of one demonstration guard, …, a contract on NEAR testnet. Each line is a purchase (or a sale) an AI agent asked for, and what the guard answered before NEAR would sign anything.

The demo's tokens live on a local fork of Base (anvil), not on Base: the traps and the buys run there, and the guard's signed transactions are broadcast only to that fork. Checks from check-mainnet.sh simulate USDC on Base mainnet, read-only, and nothing is ever sent to Base. The verdicts, checks and blocklist below are real, read from NEAR testnet.

How one check goes

  1. The agent asks. It names a token and an amount, and pays for the check.
  2. The sentinel simulates. An off-chain service runs the buy NEAR would sign, then two sales of what it bought, and judges the token.
  3. The verdict is stored on NEAR. With the sha256 of its evidence, which anyone can replay, and the hash of the one transaction the guard may sign.
  4. NEAR signs. On allow, the guard builds the swap itself and NEAR Chain Signatures signs it.
